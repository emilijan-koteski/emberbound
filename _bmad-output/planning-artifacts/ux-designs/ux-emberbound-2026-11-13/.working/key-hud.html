Key screen: in-play HUD, 1280 × 720 (itch.io embed)

Two frames, each exactly 1280 × 720. Labels sit outside the frames. The world is a flat greyscale stand-in for the 19 × 10.4-tile play box. Striped pale blocks are the bright, busy backdrop test for the HUD backing.
