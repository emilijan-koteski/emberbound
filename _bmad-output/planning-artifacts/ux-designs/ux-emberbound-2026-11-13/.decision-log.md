### Decisions proposed by facilitator, accepted without veto (2026-11-13)

Visual identity (DESIGN.md)
- D1 No final colours or typefaces in this run. DESIGN.md defines semantic colour roles, contrast rules and type roles; hex values and faces are taken from `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` at Epic 0 (CLAUDE.md: nothing visual before the style reference; renderer owns the style). Provisional greyscale hexes are allowed for the Epic 1 placeholder HUD, marked `[PROVISIONAL — replace at Epic 0]`.
- D2 UI chrome is neutral/cool. Warm colour appears in the overlay only for things that are embers (ember meter, the ember-to-heart transfer). Extends gdd.md:709 "Warm light belongs only to the hero, embers and lanterns" to the DOM overlay.
- D3 Three type roles: display (title wordmark, card text, level names on loading cards), UI (menus, labels, notices), numeric (tabular figures: clear time, counts). Constraints: self-hosted woff2, OFL-or-equivalent licence recorded in docs/ASSETS.md, ≤0.3 MB fonts total (game-architecture.md:531, 571).

HUD
- D4 Top-left cluster: hearts (3 slots), ember meter under them, weapon timer beside the meter only while a weapon is active. Nothing else persistent. No clock, no score (PROJECT_BRIEF.md:63, 67; gdd.md:587).
- D5 HUD sized in viewport units so it reads the same at any window size; inside a safe margin; never larger than needed (keep the 19 × 10.4-tile play box readable).
- D6 Last remaining heart pulses slowly (≤2 Hz). No screen-edge vignette; play area stays clear.
- D7 Speaker-off icon top-right while M-mute is on.
- Hearts: lost hearts show as empty outlines so max is always readable; hit → heart empties with a short crack/shake on the icon; restore → short pop. Ember meter: fills by length to 50; when hearts are full and meter is full it holds a steady glow ("waiting", gdd.md:581); on restore it empties into the heart (≤0.3 s transfer). Weapon timer: weapon icon (triple / piercing) + bar draining over 20 s; new pickup refills it; at 3 s left the bar blinks (≤3 Hz) in step with the warning sound (gdd.md:327).
- Warden bar placement: top-centre, narrow, with "Warden of the First Seal" (approved vocabulary) as its label; the given name is not shown (gdd.md:396). Top-centre keeps it off the floor where millstone shadows telegraph (gdd.md:413).
- HUD hidden during in-level story moments (seal break, held beat); visible during death sequence and pit fade; under the scrim while paused.

Flow and menus
- D8 Boot: minimal loading screen (wordmark + thin progress bar, no tips) while shared assets load (≤10 s to first input, gdd.md:782). Then title opens on a "Click or press any key" gate: resumes AudioContext (game-architecture.md:577), takes keyboard focus inside the itch.io iframe, sets last-used device. Then the title menu: Continue · Level select (only once unlocked) · Settings.
- D9 Pause menu: Resume · Restart level · Settings · Quit to level select. Before the level select unlocks (first Level 1 play) the last item reads "Quit to title". Restart level and Quit ask a one-step confirm with Cancel focused, because leaving mid-level restarts the level (gdd.md:801).
- D10 Settings: music volume, effects volume, reduced motion, fullscreen. Mute is the M key only, not a settings row (resolves gdd.md:765 vs gdd.md:803 / epics.md:356). Changes apply instantly and save immediately (game-architecture.md:467). Back returns to where Settings was opened (title or pause). If fullscreen is refused, the toggle snaps back and a one-line note says fullscreen isn't available here.
- D11 Results: clear time (m:ss.s), bells found x of 3, respawns, embers x of level total (gdd.md:630-634). "New best" tag when a replay beats the stored best. Values appear in a ≤0.6 s stagger; confirm skips the stagger, a second confirm continues. Respawns are shown neutrally, never framed as failure (gdd.md:209).
- D12 Cards: confirm advances one card after the ~1.5 s minimum (gdd.md:659); Back skips the rest of a sequence where skipping is allowed (intro always; closing and all-bells on replays). In-level moments keep the arch rule: jump or confirm skips, replays only (game-architecture.md:445-451).
- D13 Menu input: arrows / WASD / d-pad / left stick move focus; Enter / Space / south button confirm; Esc / Backspace / east button back. Mouse hover sets focus, click confirms; the cursor hides during play. Vertical menus wrap. Default focus is the primary action. Screen changes use fades only, no slides.
- D14 Auto-pause on: tab hidden (gdd.md:802), window blur, leaving fullscreen during play, gamepad disconnect. Held inputs are cleared on blur so Rowan doesn't keep running. Returning shows the pause menu with Resume focused (no auto-resume).
- D15 Storage refused: the title shows one line, "Progress won't be saved this session." Arch error panels unchanged ("Something went wrong" + Restart level; asset Retry panel; no technical text, game-architecture.md:700-706).
- D16 Phone/tablet note (gdd.md:797 wording) gets a "Try anyway" link for tablets with a keyboard attached.
- Loading card on replays: shows the level name only, no story line, and lasts only as long as loading takes; first plays keep the line with the ~3 s reading floor (resolves gdd.md:628 vs gdd.md:647). [ASSUMPTION — confirm with developer at draft review.]
- Level select: vertical list of finished levels plus the next unlocked one; each row shows level name, best clear time, three bell slots (found / not found). Once 9 bells are found, an "All-bells card" row appears at the bottom for rewatching (gdd.md:638). Back returns to title. Nothing locked is shown.

Accessibility and feel
- D17 Reduced motion: screen shake off (gdd.md:377) + full-screen flashes (e.g. seal-break ember flash) become a short fade + last-heart pulse and HUD icon shake stop. Hit-stop and the 9 Hz invulnerability blink stay (timing and readability; the blink is a small screen area). [NOTE FOR GDD] scope wider than gdd.md:803.
- D18 Nothing relies on colour alone: hearts are counted shapes, meters read by length, weapon timer carries an icon, bell slots are filled vs outlined. Every audio warning has a visual twin (weapon 3 s warning ↔ bar blink; warden warnings already paired, gdd.md:412-423). No large-area flash faster than 3 per second.
- D19 Fully completable keyboard-only or gamepad-only, all menus included; mouse optional. No remapping in v1 (gdd.md:257); alternate key sets (A/D or arrows; Space/Z/K; J/X) are the only flexibility.

  - **Ines**, replaying Emberwood from the level select for her ninth bell. Climax: "Bell 3 of 3", a "New best" tag on results, then the all-bells card plays after results (gdd.md:635-638) and a rewatch row appears in the level select.

Browser behaviour, tagged [ASSUMPTION — verify on the private itch.io upload, Epic 6]
- A1 Gamepad presses are not user activation under the HTML spec, so they cannot resume the AudioContext or request fullscreen. If the title gate is passed by gamepad while audio is still locked, a small line reads "Click or press a key for sound"; the game proceeds silently until then. Fullscreen toggled by gamepad may be refused → D10 snap-back note.
- A2 The Esc press that exits browser fullscreen may not reach the page; P / Start still pause, and leaving fullscreen during play auto-pauses (D14).
- A3 itch.io iframe may not have keyboard focus until clicked; the title gate text covers this.
